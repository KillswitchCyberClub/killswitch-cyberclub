Learn to break things. Then learn to fix them.

Killswitch is a student community at the Department of Mathematics, RTMNU for cybersecurity beginners and enthusiasts. We conduct workshops, play CTF competitions, and help each other build real skills.

About the club

  
We meet to learn ethical hacking, network security, cryptography, and digital forensics in a safe, legal environment. No experience needed. If you are curious, you belong here.

  

    
Workshops
Hands-on sessions on Linux, networking, web security, and tools like Wireshark and Burp Suite.

    
CTF competitions
We practice together and enter online and inter-college Capture The Flag events as a team.

    
Projects and talks
Build security tools, write blog posts, and hear from people working in the industry.
